# ResellOS

## Project Overview

ResellOS is a full-stack web application designed to help independent resellers, flippers, and small reselling businesses manage their daily operations.

Resellers often struggle with disorganized inventory records, forgotten expenses, inaccurate stock quantities, and unclear profit margins. These problems become more difficult to manage as businesses grow and sell products through multiple channels.

ResellOS aims to solve these problems by providing a centralized platform for managing products, inventory, purchases, sales, and financial performance.

## Target Users

- Independent resellers and flippers.
- Small reselling businesses.
- Online marketplace sellers.
- Resellers who sell products both online and in person.

## Minimum Viable Product (MVP)

The first version of ResellOS will focus on the essential features needed to manage a small reselling business.

### User Accounts
- Allow users to register and log in.
- Provide separate business workspaces.
- Ensure users can only access their own business information.

### Inventory Management
- Add, edit, and organize products.
- Track available inventory quantities.
- Record product purchase costs and selling prices.
- Update inventory when products are purchased or sold.

### Purchase and Sales Tracking
- Record product purchases and sales transactions.
- Track quantities, dates, and transaction prices.
- Record sales channels such as online marketplaces or in-person sales.
- Track additional expenses, including marketplace fees and shipping costs.

### Financial Tracking
- Calculate total sales revenue.
- Calculate cost of goods sold (COGS).
- Calculate estimated profit after recorded costs and fees.
- Display basic business performance metrics.

### Dashboard
- Display total revenue and estimated profit.
- Show available inventory.
- Highlight best-selling products.
- Provide a basic overview of business performance.

## Future Features

Features that may be introduced after the MVP include:

- Multiple financial account tracking.
- Automated receipt scanning using OCR.
- Marketplace integrations and cross-listing.
- Advanced sales analytics.
- Tax-oriented annual reports.
- Inventory forecasting and restocking recommendations.

## Current Development Status

ResellOS is currently in the planning and initial design stage.

No application features have been implemented yet. The current focus is requirements gathering, architecture planning, and technology evaluation.

The technology stack has not yet been finalized. Technologies will be evaluated based on project requirements, maintainability, learning value, and complexity.
